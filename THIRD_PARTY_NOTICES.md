# Third-party notices

## Tesla vehicle assets

The [Fleet resource directory](TeslaVehicleKit/Sources/TeslaVehicleFleet/Fleet/) contains models extracted from Tesla's Android app, version 4.62.5, and converted to USD for RealityKit. The experiment explored how closely RealityKit could reproduce the app's vehicle rendering and animate it from live telemetry or recorded drives.

Tesla, Inc. retains the rights in the extracted vehicle assets, including meshes, textures, materials, animations, and other data from the Tesla app. Those rights apply to the underlying assets in the converted USD and USDZ files as well. The project's [MIT license](LICENSE) covers the project-authored loader, rendering code, and app integration. The Fleet resource directory is outside the scope of that grant. Redistribution of Tesla assets, including in a compiled app, needs a separate legal basis.
